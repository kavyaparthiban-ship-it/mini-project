How CrowdSense works

From detection to recommendation to demand redistribution.

1

Detect & Predict

Simulated occupancy records are classified into Low / Moderate / High / Critical using configurable thresholds.

2

Identify Alternatives

Buses that serve the destination are found; the engine also considers realistically reachable alternatives.

3

Rank & Recommend

Scored by crowd, wait, travel, and walk (with passenger preferences). Missing/stale data is penalised.

4

Incentivise & Track

Confirm your journey to earn virtual rewards; balances and history are persisted to MySQL.
